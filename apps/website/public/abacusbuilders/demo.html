Running it

The rules that keep the cadence feeling personal, not automated.

Enter & exit

  • Enter the moment a proposal or scope is sent.
  • Pause the instant they reply or book a call — switch to a real conversation.
  • Exit on win or a clear no. Track A's "no reply" ending rolls into quarterly nurture.

Don't over-touch

  • One live person owns each thread — texts and calls come from the estimator, not a system.
  • Skip a touch if you've just spoken; never stack an SMS and a call the same day.
  • If they went silent after a warm call, shorten — don't pile on.

Texting, done right

Only text homeowners who gave you a mobile and expect to hear from you — standard for anyone who requested a bid. Keep an easy opt-out ("reply STOP") and honor it immediately. (TCPA basics.)
