# Discovery questionnaire

## Business outcome

- What result matters most?
- How is it measured today?
- What is the cost of the current problem?
- Who owns the outcome and who performs the work?

## Current process

- What triggers the process?
- What are the steps, systems, handoffs, and approval points?
- Where do delays, errors, duplicate entry, or missed follow-ups occur?
- What volume, seasonality, and service-level expectations apply?

## Data and risk

- What sensitive data is involved?
- Which systems are authoritative?
- What permissions and retention rules apply?
- Which actions require human approval?
- How can a failed change be reversed?
