# Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Scope and exclusions are approved
- [ ] Synthetic and edge-case fixtures pass
- [ ] AI output meets the documented evaluation threshold
- [ ] Authentication and permissions use least privilege
- [ ] Webhooks are verified and inputs are validated
- [ ] Retries are bounded and actions are idempotent
- [ ] Sensitive fields are redacted from logs
- [ ] Human approvals work for high-impact actions
- [ ] Alerts, recovery, rollback, and support owners are documented
- [ ] Baseline and outcome metrics are visible
- [ ] Client owner has completed acceptance testing
- [ ] Launch and post-launch review dates are agreed
