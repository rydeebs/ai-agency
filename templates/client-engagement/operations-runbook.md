# Operations runbook

## Ownership

Document the business owner, technical owner, escalation route, support hours, and dependent vendors.

## Normal operation

Describe triggers, expected volume, success signals, dashboards, cost limits, and routine maintenance.

## Failure and recovery

For each known failure mode, document detection, immediate containment, safe retry/replay, rollback, client communication, and evidence to retain.

## Change management

Record how prompts, models, schemas, permissions, and integration versions are tested and approved before production release.

## Decommissioning

Define credential revocation, data export/deletion, webhook removal, scheduled-job shutdown, and ownership transfer.
